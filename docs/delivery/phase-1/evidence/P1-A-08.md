## Acceptance contract

- `POST|PUT|DELETE /api/families/:familyId/assets` authorizes through the centralized family policy before invoking the mutation service.
- The route does not call `prisma.asset.create/update/delete` directly; all writes use the transaction-backed Balance mutation service and coordinator.
- Create, update and delete share family/actor/source/idempotency/audit scope. Update and delete use `id + familyId + version` conditional predicates, with missing `If-Match` preserving legacy compatibility by rereading the version inside the transaction.
- A completed idempotent replay returns the stored result and `Idempotency-Replayed: true`; it does not create a second Asset, AuditEvent or idempotency fact, and the family cache revision advances only for the winner.
- Viewer, non-member, malformed input and stale-version requests are rejected without Asset, audit, idempotency or family revision side effects.
